📊 Rideshare Data Analysis
This project presents two Tableau dashboards that explore rideshare activity in a selected city. Each visualization offers insights into how and where rides are happening across different times of the day and which stations are most and least used. This analysis highlights patterns that city officials and transportation planners could use to make data-driven decisions.

🚦 Dashboard 1: Interactive Map – Rides Throughout the Day
This dashboard visualizes the spatial distribution of rides throughout different hours of the day. The user can interactively filter the map using a dropdown menu that changes the hour, revealing how ride activity shifts over a 24-hour cycle.

Key insights:

Morning rush hour (7 AM–9 AM) shows increased ride activity clustered around central business areas and train stations.

Evening hours (5 PM–7 PM) see another spike, often spreading more evenly toward residential areas—suggesting a commuting pattern.

Late-night hours show significantly fewer rides, concentrated around entertainment districts or airports.

This tool can help city officials identify peak usage areas during specific times, supporting infrastructure planning and public safety strategies, such as deploying bike lanes or patrols where late-night rides are common.

📉 Dashboard 2: Top & Bottom Stations by Usage
This visualization ranks the most and least used stations based on ride counts. The user can toggle between "Top Stations" and "Bottom Stations" to get different perspectives.

Key insights:

Top stations are usually located near transportation hubs or major landmarks, indicating heavy commuter usage.

Bottom stations may represent underutilized infrastructure, which could suggest a need for better signage, accessibility improvements, or even repurposing.

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to sort the stations by direction (Start or End), which adds flexibility in identifying patterns for station placement or service demand.

🗺️ Summary for City Planning
Together, these dashboards provide a powerful snapshot of rideshare behavior across the city. By examining when and where rides are most common, and identifying which stations are being over- or under-used, local decision-makers can:

Adjust station placements for better coverage

Expand service in underserved areas

Improve infrastructure during high-traffic hours

Plan promotional campaigns or maintenance schedules for low-traffic stations

All insights are delivered through intuitive visuals and interactivity, allowing non-technical stakeholders to explore the data firsthand.
